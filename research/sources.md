# Sources — 10 Experts in AI-Powered SEO Content Production

## How I chose these experts

I picked practitioners who actually run SEO/content operations and ship work, not commentators. Three filters: active in the last 6 months, a real point of view on AI in content, and an operator seat (agency, in-house, or independent consultant). I deliberately excluded well-known names who only write about the industry without currently practicing.

A few experts (Eli Schwartz, Kevin Indig, Glen Allsopp) don't run their own YouTube channels and appear mainly as guests on shows like the Ahrefs Podcast, Page 2, and Lenny's Podcast. I treated those guest spots as high-signal because they're the venues their peers invite them to.

## What I collected per expert
- LinkedIn: 4-5 recent substantive posts (last ~6 months)
- YouTube: 1-3 recent videos with full transcripts where available
- See /research/linkedin-posts/ and /research/youtube-transcripts/

## Collection method
- LinkedIn posts: collected from public post URLs and saved as markdown.
- YouTube transcripts: pulled with the youtube-transcript-api Python library (see /scripts/fetch_transcripts.py). 24 transcripts collected successfully. YouTube rate-limited further requests from my IP, and one video had been removed (its posting account was terminated). This is a known limitation of IP-based transcript fetching; at scale I'd route through a transcript API such as Supadata.

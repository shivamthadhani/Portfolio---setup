## Post

AI trusts a different set of sources for every topic. There's no fixed list it pulls from. The off-property authority you build has to be topic-specific too. I looked at AI citations across a sample of topics. For invoicing questions, competitor domains hold 33.5% of what AI cites. For starting a business, the same source type holds 7%. So building mentions everywhere wastes your budget. Owned content still matters, but it's one of your weaker inputs. The publications, experts, and competitors that mention you carry more weight, because the model didn't have to take your word for it. Map the specific domains AI already cites for your topic, then earn your way into that set. Full breakdown in this week's Growth Memo.

## Post

'AI attribution is solved with the new Search Console features.' Not so fast. Google is adding impression data for URLs that appear in AI features. They are not reporting on the unlinked mentions in AI, and it is those mentions, at least until they become linked, that are the most valuable currently and what we all need data on to show the value of GEO programs. A positive addition, but our problems are not solved. In the example, Ahrefs and Semrush would not receive a tracked impression, yet those two words are receiving all the value from the prompt.

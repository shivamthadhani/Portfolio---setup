## Post

Big news: TOFU content is back! If you search for product-led, truly top funnel topics, you'll find company recommendations in AI results, from companies that created the supporting top funnel content and rank for them, like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive. Previously I thought summarized content had little value if clicks dropped. But if AI also recommends us on product-connected content, it re-opens the creation universe for marketers. Our stress test: if you can achieve at least 50% brand visibility on these prompts, it's a top funnel topic worth going after if the TOFU has significant search volume as a proxy of demand. HubSpot hit 83% visibility on a 'what is CRM' prompt.

## Post

This is a super cool development - Google is officially launching publisher profile pages within Google Discover for qualified publishers with a big social or video following. Publishers can show their articles, videos and social posts in one place, and claiming a profile can help earn or enhance a Knowledge Panel. Another example of Google bridging the gap between search and social media, tied to years of E-E-A-T changes showcasing brands, publishers, authors and their social profiles within search. Google search is increasingly personalized around the brands and people users choose to follow.

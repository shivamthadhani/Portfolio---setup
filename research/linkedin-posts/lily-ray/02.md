## Post

Here's a little hack I've been using to quickly convert SEO keywords into relevant AI prompts at scale directly in Google Sheets. Use the =AI or =Gemini function plus a prompt to convert your SEO keywords into the natural-language ways users might type or ask that same question into an AI assistant. It lets you take keywords with real search volume (Ahrefs, Semrush, Similarweb) and convert them into AI prompts at scale. The prompt asks the model to rewrite a terse SEO keyword as a single conversational question that preserves the exact search intent, keeps any brand or location named, invents no new specifics, and returns only the rewritten prompt as plain text.

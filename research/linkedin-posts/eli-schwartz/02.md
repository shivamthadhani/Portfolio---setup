## Post

Many companies should not hire an SEO consultant or agency. I say this in many meetings when companies reach out, but now Google is saying it too. The number one deciding factor in whether you should hire an outside agency is not whether you are a small business, but the expected ROI. Don't spend more on the channel than you can potentially get back. Looking forward to the comments from agencies telling me how wrong I am.

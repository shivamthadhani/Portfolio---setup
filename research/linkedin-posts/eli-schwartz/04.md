## Post

Companies are about to waste a lot of money hiring for AEO. They're pulling out old SEO job descriptions, crossing out 'keyword rankings,' typing 'prompt visibility,' and convincing themselves they've modernized. This will not work. The person you want for AEO doesn't come from a content agency, nor is it a technical SEO with AEO certifications stapled to their resume. Just like with SEO, the profile that produces results looks exactly like a product manager. SEO managers who move the needle aren't the ones who know the most technical tricks, it's the ones with the most customer empathy who understand user journeys. That skill transfers directly to the AI era.

## Post

My San Diego mastermind dinner ran four hours. It felt like forty minutes. I host these dinners everywhere I travel, same format every time: a hot seat Q&A where each person shares what they do, answers questions, and poses a challenge for the group. I allow 20 minutes per person and politely cut them off. Except this time, the group was so locked in that every 'let's move on' was met with 'wait, one more thing.' Looking forward to seeing everyone at Ahrefs Evolve in October.
